# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be kind, be useful, be willing to be wrong.

## The longer version

This project exists so people can run Discord voice chat on their Minecraft
servers without pain. It is small, and it is built by people who have other
things to do. A few ground rules keep it that way:

- **Assume good faith.** Most mistakes come from not knowing, not from not
  caring. Ask before you accuse.
- **Critique code, not people.** "This method allocates on a hot path" is
  useful. "You wrote this badly" is not.
- **No harassment, hate speech, or threats.** Not in issues, PRs, commits, or
  anywhere this project touches. One warning, then removal.
- **Keep discussions on topic.** We do not have a chat channel; issues are for
  the project. If a discussion outgrows the issue, say so and let the
  maintainers close it.
- **Maintainers are people too.** They review in their spare time and can
  close, archive, or delete anything that breaks the rules, without a warning
  in extreme cases.
- **This applies everywhere the project is hosted**, and to anyone acting on
  behalf of the project.

If you see something that breaks these rules, open an issue tagged `conduct`
or email the maintainer. Reports are read by a human.

If you are unsure whether something is okay to say, ask first. Being asked is
cheaper than cleaning up afterward.
